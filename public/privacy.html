Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

What we collect

We collect your email address, organisation name, and domain names you add to CyberGuard. We store scan results — which are based entirely on publicly available data about your domain. If you upload staff email addresses for breach checking, these are stored encrypted.

What we don't collect

We do not collect passwords (Supabase handles authentication). We do not track browsing behaviour beyond your use of CyberGuard. We do not sell your data to third parties.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the CyberGuard service — running scans, sending alerts, and showing you your security dashboard. We send transactional emails (scan alerts, weekly digest) using Resend. You can opt out of all non-essential emails in Settings.

Data storage

Data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on AWS infrastructure in the EU. Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Account Settings. You can request a data export by emailing us. Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data.

Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@cyberguard.app
